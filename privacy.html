Genergo Privacy Policy for Internship Candidates

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Genergo (“Genergo”, “we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal data of candidates who apply for internships or respond to internship vacancies. It applies to applications submitted through our website, email, job platforms, applicant tracking systems, social media, referrals, or other channels.

Genergo is a startup based in Kazakhstan. We process personal data in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan “On Personal Data and their Protection” No. 94-V dated 21 May 2013 (as amended), and we aim to apply these rules to candidates worldwide.

1. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for your personal data is:

Genergo
Operating as a startup based in Kazakhstan
Email: genergoai@gmail.com

2. Personal Data We Collect

  • Identification and contact data: name, email address, phone number, location, LinkedIn profile, portfolio, GitHub, or other links.
  • Application data: CV/resume, cover letter, education, work or internship experience, skills, languages, certificates, references, availability, preferred start date, and salary expectations.
  • Communication data: emails, messages, interview notes, feedback, and other correspondence with you.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, referral source, and cookies or similar technologies where applicable.

We do not collect diversity or special category data, biometric data, or data from candidates under 18 years of age.

3. How We Collect Your Data

  • directly from you when you submit an application or communicate with us;
  • from platforms and services we use for recruitment, including Google Forms, Notion, Telegram, Email, Astanahub, GitHub, Habr, Proglib, and other job platforms;
  • automatically through our website or application systems;
  • from publicly available professional sources, such as GitHub or professional networks, where relevant to recruitment.

4. Purposes and Legal Bases

  • Recruitment and selection: to review your application, assess your qualifications, and communicate with you. Legal basis: legitimate interests and/or steps prior to entering a contract.
  • Communication: to respond to your questions and inform you about your application status. Legal basis: legitimate interests and/or consent.
  • Compliance: to comply with legal, tax, or regulatory obligations under the laws of the Republic of Kazakhstan. Legal basis: legal obligation.
  • Analytics and improvement: to improve our recruitment process and website. Legal basis: legitimate interests and/or consent.
  • Talent pool: to consider you for future internship opportunities. Legal basis: consent, where required.

5. Sharing Your Data

We may share your personal data with:

  • service providers that help us with recruitment, hosting, email, cloud storage, analytics, and applicant tracking, including Google (Forms), Notion, Telegram, and other platforms we use;
  • Genergo employees and contractors involved in recruitment;
  • professional advisers, such as lawyers or accountants, when necessary;
  • legal authorities, courts, or regulators when required by law, including the authorized body of the Republic of Kazakhstan for personal data protection;
  • a buyer or successor in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

We do not sell your personal data.

6. International Transfers

Because candidates may be located worldwide and we use international services, your personal data may be transferred outside Kazakhstan. We will use appropriate safeguards for such transfers, including ensuring that the receiving jurisdiction provides adequate protection or by obtaining your consent where required by the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan No. 94-V.

7. Data Retention

We retain candidate data for 5 years after the application process ends, unless you withdraw your consent earlier or request deletion. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it. If you are hired, your data may become part of your employee record.

8. Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Your Rights

Under the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan No. 94-V, you have the right to:

  • access your personal data;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • request deletion of your data;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time;
  • lodge a complaint with the authorized body for personal data protection in Kazakhstan.

To exercise your rights, contact us at genergoai@gmail.com.

10. Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

11. Automated Decision-Making

We do not make solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you. We do not use automated tools to screen applications.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
